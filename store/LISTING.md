## English (en-US)

**Name:** The Ultraspeaker Image Lab

**Summary:** Fix slide photos in PowerPoint with a histogram, levels, light and colour. The original is kept.

**Description:**
The Ultraspeaker Image Lab brings photo-editor controls into PowerPoint, so the images in your slides look their best without leaving your presentation.

Select a photo and open Image Lab from the Home tab:
- A live histogram (RGB, single channels or luminance) with three level sliders: black at 0%, midtones at 50%, white at 100%.
- Clipping warnings when shadows or highlights lose detail.
- Exposure, contrast, saturation, highlights, shadows, sharpness and noise reduction.
- Temperature and tint, plus a neutral-point picker that fixes colour casts with one click.
- Quick styles: Natural, Stage, Black & white, Warm and Enhance.
- Before / After comparison in the pane.

Non-destructive: when you apply, the adjusted photo takes the place of the original with the same position, size and rotation. The original stays on the slide, hidden, together with your settings. Select the photo again to change the adjustments or go back to the original at any time.

Private by design: photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded.

Available in English, Italian, Spanish, French and German.

Requires Microsoft 365 PowerPoint on Windows (version 2601 or later), Mac (16.105 or later) or PowerPoint on the web.

**Search keywords:** photo, histogram, levels
